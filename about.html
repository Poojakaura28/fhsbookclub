About

A book club you can actually walk into.

We read books and we talk about them. That’s the core of it. Around that, we do a few book-related things so it isn’t only discussion.

Who it’s for

The club is for FHS students. If you read a book a week, great. If you read one a year, also great. You don’t need to be a “reader” to sit in on a conversation about a book.

What we do

Most of it is discussion: what we liked, what we didn’t, what we’d argue about. About once a month we aim to do something more hands-on, like the activities below.

We’re also planning to promote events at the local library. That’s a plan, not a done deal. Nothing is confirmed and no dates are set, and we’ll update the Events page when that changes.

Things we might do

Six activities on our list

Book bedazzling is our first one. The rest are ones we’ve discussed, and each is an idea until it shows up on the Events page as confirmed.

  1. Book bedazzling

    Gems, paint pens and stickers on a notebook or a book you own. Not library books.

    This is our first event. See the Events page for the date.

  2. Book bingo

    A bingo card of reading prompts, like “a book with a one-word title.” Cross off squares as you read.

    We’ve discussed this one. Nothing is scheduled yet.

  3. Bookmark making

    Make a bookmark, keep it, lose it inside a library book, make another.

    We’ve discussed this one. Nothing is scheduled yet.

  4. Blind-date-with-a-book

    Books wrapped in paper with a few clues on the outside. You pick one without seeing the cover.

    We’ve discussed this one. Nothing is scheduled yet.

  5. Buddy reading

    Pair up, read the same book, and check in about it halfway through.

    We’ve discussed this one. Nothing is scheduled yet.

  6. Book swaps

    Bring a book you’re done with and take home one you haven’t read.

    We’ve discussed this one. Nothing is scheduled yet.
